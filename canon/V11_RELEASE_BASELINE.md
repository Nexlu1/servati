This deliberately narrow baseline records only what the repository itself
established before V12 development:

- The project is titled **SERVATI: The Worlds That Held the Dead**.
- It was created by Marcus Frost.
- It is a speculative-evolution and future-history project exploring memory,
  continuity, obligation, and the long aftermath of humanity.
- The Continuance was originally created to preserve the dead.
- Civilisations, ecologies, and post-human descendants emerge around the
  Continuance over immense spans of time.
- **Version 11 — Differentiation and Deep-Time Expansion Edition** is the
  current release.

This is not a substitute for the Version 11 manuscript. No detailed chronology,
terminology, lineage list, or page-specific claim is promoted here because the
manuscript is not present in the repository.
