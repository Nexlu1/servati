# Version 11 Core Terms

- **Canon status:** CORE
- **Source verification:** VERIFIED
- **Source:** [Authoritative Version 11 manuscript, Appendix
  I](../SERVATI_V11_AUTHORITATIVE_MANUSCRIPT.md#appendix-i---core-terms)

These concise definitions reproduce the terms used by the V12 foundation. The
manuscript remains authoritative where a summary loses nuance.

- **Servati** — Latin forms meaning the kept, preserved, guarded, or those held
  from disappearance. It names the book, not a single faction, and does not
  mean salvation.
- **Continuance** — the linked system of preservation, custody, identity,
  routing, chambers, records, seeds, machines, and inherited obligations that
  grows from the original crisis response.
- **burden** — the preserved physical dead: body, fragment, capsule, ash,
  residue, or other matter-state requiring custody.
- **custody** — holding without consuming, erasing, falsely resolving,
  misnaming, or abandoning.
- **Reliquary Earth** — Earth after its conversion into a planetary
  preservation ecology.
- **Scattered Lamp** — an isolated continuity centre preserving part of the
  inheritance after the shared horizon fails.
- **inheritance split** — the division of the Continuance into six dominant
  preservation logics: **Remains**, **Memory**, **Seed**, **Ritual**,
  **Structure**, and **Pattern**.
- **Archive Faith** — a sacred identity system formed around one preservation
  logic, method, or inherited obligation.
- **Custody War** — conflict over rightful preservation, access, relocation,
  interpretation, or release of burdens, records, seeds, structures, routes,
  or patterns.
- **Great Misreading** — the period and condition in which humanity survives as
  evidence but not as one complete understanding.
- **Choir** — a large custodial civilisation formed when Scattered Lamps
  reconnect, coordinate, or enter sustained relation.
- **Shrine World** — a planet or major body reorganised around one dominant
  preservation function.
- **Outer Custody** — a remote reserve withdrawn from active worlds into cold,
  distance, and silence.
- **Luminous Remnant** — custody or witness persisting through emission, signal,
  swarm, or light-field after local structures fail.
- **Black Shrine** — a withheld custody structure protecting irreducible,
  dangerous, shaming, or unresolved remnants from false resolution.
- **Great Thinning** — the late era when routes, signals, worlds, and archives
  cease answering one another.
- **Last Maintenance** — the final scale of care, narrowed from civilisation to
  one local act still due.
- **Last Census** — the terminal accounting of the Continuance, distinguishing
  **Held**, **Lost**, **Unreachable**, **Unresolved**, **Withheld**, **Still
  Emitting**, and **Uncountable**.
