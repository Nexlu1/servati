# Chronology Control

The repository does not yet contain a source-verified Version 11 chronology.
The ordered stage list supplied by the V12 working pack is preserved in
`../research/v11-source-review/v11_candidate_seed.yaml` with
`SOURCE-UNAVAILABLE` status.

Until the manuscript can be inspected:

- no working-pack stage name is promoted to CORE;
- no numerical deep-time date is assigned;
- DRAFT entries may use a claimed era only when their provenance states that
  the source is unavailable; and
- ordering, durations, and dates require both source comparison and appropriate
  astronomy or cosmology review before promotion.

Uncertainty should be represented explicitly with the chronology precision
values defined by `../schemas/servati-dataset.schema.json`.
