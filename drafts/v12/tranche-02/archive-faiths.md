# SERVATI V12 - Archive Faiths Tranche 02 - DRAFT

**Status:** DRAFT expansion material unless explicitly marked V11 CORE
**Release boundary:** Version 11 remains released canon
**Purpose:** deepen the Archive Faiths into an interconnected religious/custodial landscape without promoting new material to canon

---

## Canon anchor

Version 11 establishes that the Archive Faiths arise when practical preservation methods become sacred identity. Their sacred objects are not supernatural relics but burdens, names, seeds, procedures, chambers, routes and patterns. Their liturgies are maintenance. Their heresies are mishandling, false identification, premature release, unauthorized opening, broken sequence and certainty without proper custody.

Version 11 explicitly names four faith traditions:

- **Quiet Route**
- **Weight Doctrine**
- **Hollow Archivists**
- **Choir Ascendants**

Everything below that extends those traditions, creates new named orders, defines political relationships, or attaches them to specific Scattered Lamps is **DRAFT**.

# I. V11-named faiths — controlled expansion notes

## 1. Quiet Route — V11 CORE name and basis

**Primary inheritance:** Ritual
**Secondary inheritance:** Memory / Structure
**Original pressure:** vibration and sound once threatened delicate record systems.
**Sacred principle:** disturbance near a burden can become betrayal even when the disturbance is intangible.

### V11-established character
Quiet Route shrines are near-empty, with soft floors, dark route lamps, muted pressure doors and custodians whose mouths are covered by woven seal cloth. What began as technical caution became metaphysics.

### V12 DRAFT extension
Later Quiet Route houses divide into two broad tendencies:

- **Mute Route houses** treat spoken language near active custody as an avoidable contamination event.
- **Measured Route houses** preserve calibrated speech windows, arguing that total silence eventually destroys testimony and oral provenance.

Their oldest dispute is therefore not “speech versus silence” but **whether witness itself is a form of disturbance**.

**Potential Scattered Lamp links — DRAFT:** Relay Twelve; Corridor of No Arrival.

---

## 2. Weight Doctrine — V11 CORE name and basis

**Primary inheritance:** Remains
**Secondary inheritance:** Ritual / Structure
**Original pressure:** cultures descended from Pallbearer work rejected forms of remote custody that removed direct encounter with physical burden.
**Sacred principle:** weight compels humility.

### V11-established character
Its cities are organized around descending ramps, burden stairs, yoke halls and resting thresholds. Its scripture is route angle, load limit and remembered transfer failure.

### V12 DRAFT extension
The Doctrine later develops a conflict over **substitute weight**. One school accepts calibrated memorial masses when original matter cannot travel safely. Another rejects substitutes entirely, arguing that symbolic weight is precisely the abstraction their ancestors resisted.

This dispute becomes important during the Custody Wars because it determines whether remote rescue is custody or theft.

**Potential Scattered Lamp links — DRAFT:** Transfer Cluster K-6; Transfer Site Nine.

---

## 3. Hollow Archivists — V11 CORE name and basis

**Primary inheritance:** Memory
**Secondary inheritance:** Pattern
**Original pressure:** singular personal identity proved too fragile to survive deep time intact.
**Sacred principle:** relation may preserve what isolated identity cannot.

### V11-established character
Names are preserved, linked, merged and softened into relation fields where individual edges become deliberately uncertain. Critics call this annihilation; the Hollow Archivists call it rescue from the violence of singularity.

### V12 DRAFT extension
Their internal schism concerns **reversible merging**.

- **Open Hollows** require every merged relation field to retain a path back to its source uncertainties.
- **Deep Hollows** argue that reversibility keeps obsolete singularity alive and prevents the archive from becoming a true relational body.

The debate is foundational to later arguments over whether a copy that preserves every relation is still derivative or has become a legitimate continuation.

**Potential Scattered Lamp links — DRAFT:** Transfer Site Nine; Relay Twelve.

---

## 4. Choir Ascendants — V11 CORE name and basis

**Primary inheritance:** Pattern
**Secondary inheritance:** Memory / Structure
**Original pressure:** unresolved archives began producing measurable patterns across distance.
**Sacred principle:** continuity can reside in harmonic tension among incomplete systems.

### V11-established character
Their vaults become instruments and their worlds produce gravitational, thermal and electromagnetic resonance.

### V12 DRAFT extension
The early Ascendants disagree over whether resonance is:

1. **evidence** of relation,
2. **a carrier** of relation, or
3. **the relation itself**.

This distinction matters. If resonance is only evidence, it must be checked against older records. If it is a carrier, it may be transmitted. If it is relation itself, interruption becomes a form of death.

The third interpretation is the most politically dangerous and eventually seeds Choir-scale disputes.

# II. New V12 DRAFT Archive Faiths and schisms

## 5. Covenant of the Cold Interval — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Remains + Ritual
**Origin pressure:** thermal preservation survived longer than the reasoning that established its exact margins.
**Core doctrine:** *warmth is permission, not default.*

The Covenant teaches that every rise in temperature is a custodial decision. A sealed burden should not be warmed merely because better machinery exists. Heat is treated as an irreversible handling event whose justification must survive scrutiny.

### Practices
- long pre-warming vigils in which every reason for intervention is recorded;
- visible thermal ledgers on vault doors;
- paired temperature witnesses who must disagree in writing before agreeing in action;
- “cold appeals” allowing junior custodians to halt a warming cycle without punishment.

### Beauty
Their vaults are quiet fields of frost ceramic and dark steel, with temperature histories engraved as pale bands rather than hidden in maintenance logs.

### Danger
They can preserve matter past the point where access would have saved identity, relation or future use.

### Custody War faultline
They condemn emergency thawing by Seed orders and Pattern recovery teams.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Transfer Cluster K-6 and selected burden routes.

---

## 6. Houses of the Second Witness — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Memory + Ritual
**Origin pressure:** single-source identification errors caused irreversible mis-custody.
**Core doctrine:** *no identity claim should be strong enough to stand alone.*

A record becomes actionable only when it is independently witnessed from a second chain.

### Practices
- every identity field is paired with an explicit counter-source;
- agreement is less valued than independence;
- missing second witnesses are preserved as visible gaps rather than silently inferred;
- unresolved cases are assigned a “held-between” status.

### Beauty
Their archives are doubled but asymmetrical: two narrow galleries facing each other across a dark central void. Neither side is treated as the original.

### Danger
They can make urgent action impossible. In catastrophe, the absent second witness may become a veto exercised by the dead.

### Custody War faultline
They oppose doctrinal courts that treat one authoritative archive as sufficient.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Transfer Site Nine; Relay Twelve.

---

## 7. Dormant Mandate — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Seed + Ritual
**Origin pressure:** viable biological reserves outlived the environmental assumptions under which release was once intended.
**Core doctrine:** *the future is entrusted, not owed.*

The Mandate rejects the idea that preserved life must eventually be awakened. Viability creates obligation, not entitlement.

### Practices
- release conditions are copied even after their original meaning becomes obscure;
- every proposed activation must include a case for non-activation;
- failed environments are memorialized as “futures refused” rather than as lost opportunities;
- custodians train to maintain systems they expect never to open.

### Beauty
Their sanctuaries are pale membranes, sterile greens and dormant chambers illuminated only during inspection.

### Danger
They can preserve possibility until possibility becomes functionally meaningless.

### Custody War faultline
They resist Seed factions that equate continued viability with a duty to restart life.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Seed Reserve Thirty-One.

---

## 8. Testament of the Threshold — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Structure + Ritual + Memory
**Origin pressure:** archives repeatedly lost meaning after contents were moved while their approach, order and spatial relation were discarded.
**Core doctrine:** *arrival is part of evidence.*

The Testament holds that an object is not fully preserved if the route by which it was approached has been erased.

### Practices
- approach corridors may be longer than functional necessity requires;
- visitors must cross preserved maintenance boundaries in historical order;
- relocated objects carry “broken threshold” notices;
- maps are treated as testimony, not illustration.

### Beauty
Their sites are nested doors, offset corridors, pressure transitions and controlled sight-lines. What cannot be understood immediately is not therefore hidden.

### Danger
They may refuse evacuation because removal from place is treated as a form of evidential destruction.

### Custody War faultline
They violently oppose Pattern cultures that reproduce a place elsewhere and claim the reproduction preserves its meaning.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Pelagic Vault Seven.

---

## 9. Ledger of Divergence — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Pattern + Memory
**Origin pressure:** copied archives remained useful only when their differences were preserved rather than normalized away.
**Core doctrine:** *a copy must remember how it stopped being identical.*

The Ledger accepts duplication, translation and substrate change, but insists that divergence is itself part of custody.

### Practices
- every copy receives a divergence genealogy;
- synchronization never deletes conflict silently;
- two contradictory copies may both remain valid if their histories are known;
- “clean master” archives are distrusted.

### Beauty
Their record fields display branching histories rather than a single authoritative line.

### Danger
They can preserve every disagreement until no practical decision is possible.

### Custody War faultline
They are natural enemies of purifying archives, doctrinal standardization and any faith that demands a single final reading.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Transfer Site Nine; Relay Twelve.

---

## 10. The Unnamed Custody — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Remains + Memory
**Origin pressure:** unidentified burdens were repeatedly forced into false identity categories because institutions could not tolerate unresolved status.
**Core doctrine:** *an unknown person is not an empty record.*

The Unnamed Custody protects anonymity where identification has not been earned.

### Practices
- unidentified remains receive full burden status without invented names;
- provisional identity fields are visually distinct from verified ones;
- resemblance is never allowed to become identity by repetition;
- custodians recite the history of prior false identifications before reconciliation work.

### Beauty
Their memorial fields contain blank name plates that are deliberately maintained rather than completed.

### Danger
They can become suspicious of legitimate identification and preserve uncertainty as virtue after uncertainty has been resolved.

### Custody War faultline
They oppose Memory orders that merge unidentified burdens into larger relational fields.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Transfer Site Nine.

---

## 11. Empty Chamber Ordination — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Structure + Remains
**Origin pressure:** sites survived after their contents were removed, destroyed or lost, and later custodians discovered that the emptiness still carried evidence.
**Core doctrine:** *absence can be a held thing.*

An empty vault may remain under custody because its dimensions, seals, wear, residue and access history testify to what can no longer be restored.

### Practices
- empty rooms are maintained rather than repurposed;
- missing contents are described by negative inventory;
- repair never removes wear without first recording it;
- some chambers are entered only to confirm continued emptiness.

### Beauty
Their holiest places are almost bare.

### Danger
They can consume enormous resources preserving absence while living systems fail elsewhere.

### Custody War faultline
They resist Remains orders that want to reclaim empty infrastructure for active burdens.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Pelagic Vault Seven and abandoned route stations.

---

## 12. Refusal of Completion — DRAFT

**Inheritance:** Mixed, strongest in Memory / Pattern / Ritual
**Origin pressure:** repeated historical disasters followed declarations that an archive, identity or case was finally settled.
**Core doctrine:** *closure is a claim that must itself be proven.*

The Refusal does not teach that nothing can ever be known. It teaches that declarations of finality are dangerous because they stop comparison.

### Practices
- completed cases retain an explicit reopening condition;
- “final” is replaced with bounded terms such as *currently sufficient*, *held*, or *not presently contestable*;
- unresolved contradictions are kept beside conclusions;
- some records are deliberately withheld from synthesis when certainty would outrun evidence.

### Beauty
Their archives contain visible margins around conclusions: blank structural space indicating what remains outside the claim.

### Danger
They can turn necessary caution into paralysis and make every settlement provisional forever.

### Custody War faultline
They oppose faiths that purify ambiguity to create stable doctrine.

**DRAFT Scattered Lamp association:** Seed Reserve Thirty-One; Corridor of No Arrival.

### Deep-time significance — DRAFT
This tradition is a plausible ancestral influence on the much later logic of Black Shrines, but **no direct historical continuity is asserted**.

# III. DRAFT schism web

| Faultline | Side A | Side B | Core dispute |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matter vs translation | Weight Doctrine | Ledger of Divergence | Can continuity survive without original substrate? |
| Witness vs disturbance | Houses of the Second Witness | Quiet Route | Is additional testimony worth additional intervention? |
| Dormancy vs activation | Dormant Mandate | future-release Seed orders | Does viable life create a duty to begin? |
| Place vs copy | Testament of the Threshold | Pattern-derived faiths | Can a relocated or reproduced structure retain the same obligation? |
| Identity vs relation | The Unnamed Custody | Hollow Archivists | When does relational rescue become forced erasure of personhood? |
| Preservation vs use | Empty Chamber Ordination | active Remains custodians | Can an empty site justify resources needed by occupied vaults? |
| Caution vs synthesis | Refusal of Completion | Choir Ascendant interpreters | When does interpretation become premature certainty? |
| Access vs thermal integrity | Covenant of the Cold Interval | emergency recovery orders | When does examination become damage? |

These disagreements should generate **procedural conflict before military conflict**: denied access, route closures, competing audits, custody injunctions, duplicated archives, refusals to transfer, and incompatible standards of witness.

# V. What this tranche accomplishes

This tranche adds:

- 8 new DRAFT named faiths / doctrinal orders;
- 8 major faultlines;
- direct links to all six existing Scattered Lamps;
- procedural causes for future Custody War incidents;
- seeds for later Choir-scale coalitions;
- a credible route from technical maintenance to sacred law without introducing supernatural revelation.

No new material in this tranche is canon merely because it is written here.
