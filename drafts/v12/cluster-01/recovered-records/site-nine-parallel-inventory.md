## DRAFT editorial reading

The fragment supports the Concordance's refusal but does not make that refusal
infallible. A missing outer witness channel may once have contained evidence
that justified separation. Filling that blank with a sympathetic voice would
manufacture moral certainty.

The preservation problem is therefore not only physical. The neat survival of
three columns tempts readers to treat them as the complete field when the
record itself proves at least one channel is gone.
