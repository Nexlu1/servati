# DRAFT — The Sixfold Custody Fracture

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Sequence type:** proposed relative order
- **Absolute chronology:** none
- **Canon boundary:** Version 11 supports the conflict types but neither this
  grouping nor this order

This sequence turns the [six existing DRAFT Custody War
incidents](../tranche-01/custody-war-incidents.md) into one history of damaged
comparison. The connections are deliberately institutional and evidentiary.
No conquering empire, singular enemy, chosen protagonist, or conventional
campaign is introduced.

The order below is a working hypothesis. Position means only “before or after
within this proposed chain.” It supplies no year, duration, interval, or claim
that the events were the only things happening between its stages.

## 1. The Silence Breach of Relay Twelve

The sequence begins when an outside custody order treats Relay Twelve's quiet
as evidence of abandonment and forces a stronger witness transmission. The
local keepers can demonstrate risk to their relation fields; the outside order
can demonstrate identities made unreachable by silence. Some records pass.
Some comparison structure is damaged. Both sides leave with real evidence.

**Proposed bridge:** The dispute makes inaccessible uncertainty politically
suspect. Copies that answer cleanly begin to look safer than archives that
answer slowly and conditionally.

**Internal view:** [Quiet-Line Transcript
Twelve-B](recovered-records/quiet-line-transcript.md)

## 2. The Index Without Uncertainty

A copied identity index resolves ambiguity by omitting uncertainty fields. It
is easier to transmit, compare, and act upon than the injured or inaccessible
sources. Its usefulness accelerates adoption before custodians understand what
has been removed.

**Proposed bridge:** The index becomes part of remote authority checks. It does
not have to be a deliberate superweapon; ordinary reliance on a convenient,
internally consistent copy is enough to spread harm.

## 3. The False Release at Seed Reserve Thirty-One

Reserve Thirty-One receives an order whose procedure looks valid while its
provenance fails. A simplified identity or authority chain associated with the
uncertainty-stripped index helps the order appear complete. The reserve refuses
release, but its keepers also record that continued dormancy has biological and
moral costs.

**Proposed bridge:** The refusal breaks trust in remote authorisation. Other
custody orders respond by asserting more direct control over routes and held
matter.

**Internal view:** [Refusal Pair
Thirty-One](recovered-records/refusal-pair-thirty-one.md)

## 4. The Redirection of the Ash Convoy

Two authorities claim the same sealed burdens. Neither wishes to violate the
load, so control is exercised through gradients, resting thresholds, sledge
custody, and the definition of lawful continuation. The matter survives while
the route record splits.

**Proposed bridge:** K-6 sends both route histories to Site Nine because no
single Weight-derived practice can compare them. The act of external comparison
is then used by rivals as evidence that K-6 has surrendered custody.

**Internal view:** [Ash Convoy Handoff
Leaf](recovered-records/ash-convoy-handoff-leaf.md)

## 5. The Drowned Marker Seizure

A rival authority removes Pelagic route markers from claimed towers. The
objects survive. Their depth, current, approach, and relation do not. Pelagic
custodians transfer at least one displaced marker to Site Nine under a record
that explicitly refuses to call the relocation restoration.

**Proposed bridge:** Site Nine now holds matter from K-6, displaced Pelagic
context, incomplete identity records, and pattern comparisons. Its mixed
holdings become a test case for every faction that wants doctrinal clarity.

## 6. The Partition of Transfer Site Nine

Three authorities demand that Site Nine separate material, memorial, and
pattern holdings. The Concordance argues that the relations among them are also
evidence. Resistance does not save the comparison rack intact. Most objects
survive, but the means by which they corrected one another is injured.

**Outcome:** The six Lamps establish the DRAFT Six-Lamp Correction Circuit,
not because they agree, but because each has now suffered a failure its own
inheritance cannot diagnose alone.

**Internal view:** [Site Nine Parallel Inventory
Fragment](recovered-records/site-nine-parallel-inventory.md)

## What remains unresolved

- The identity and continuity of the outside order at Relay Twelve are not
  defined.
- It is not settled whether omission in the index was deliberate, negligent,
  or produced by a translation boundary.
- No surviving evidence proves the false release order depended on that exact
  index copy; the link is DRAFT.
- The two Ash Convoy authorities are not yet separately particularised.
- The rival claim over the Pelagic towers is unspecified.
- “Three authorities” at Site Nine describes functional claims, not yet three
  named polities or armies.
- Spatial distance and message or travel delay among all sites remain unknown.

These gaps are preserved because filling them would create new certainty rather
than new depth.
