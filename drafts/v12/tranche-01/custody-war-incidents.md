# DRAFT — Custody War Incidents, Tranche 01

These incidents turn verified Version 11 conflict types into named candidate
history. They are not canon. Each basis cites [Chapter 21 of the authoritative
manuscript](../../../SERVATI_V11_AUTHORITATIVE_MANUSCRIPT.md#chapter-21---the-custody-wars),
with Chapter 18 noted where relevant. Verification applies only to the stated
basis; every named incident and narrative particular remains **DRAFT**.

A connected but still DRAFT relative ordering is proposed in [The Sixfold
Custody Fracture](../cluster-01/custody-fracture-sequence.md). Version 11 does
not supply that ordering.

## The Partition of Transfer Site Nine

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Type:** Custody War incident

**Verified V11 basis:** mixed sites became contested and were divided,
purified, or destroyed during the Custody Wars.

Three custody authorities attempt to separate a mixed relic complex into
matter, record, and pattern holdings. The Concordance refuses because the
relations among the fragments are themselves evidence. The attempted partition
damages the comparison field even though most physical material survives.

**Linked site:** [The Broken Frame Concordance at Transfer Site
Nine](scattered-lamps.md#the-broken-frame-concordance-at-transfer-site-nine)

**Consequence:** A candidate example of why preserving objects while severing
relations can still constitute catastrophic loss.

## The False Release at Seed Reserve Thirty-One

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Type:** Custody War incident

**Verified V11 basis:** V11 separately describes false release orders as worse
than breaches and a seed reserve opened by one doctrine but mourned by another
as murder. Their combination here is DRAFT.

A release authorisation reaches the reserve carrying valid-looking procedural
structure but incompatible provenance. One faction argues that delay is killing
the future; another argues that activation under false authority would be an
irreversible violation.

**Linked site:** [The Unopened Spring of Seed Reserve Thirty-One](scattered-lamps.md#the-unopened-spring-of-seed-reserve-thirty-one)

**Consequence:** The reserve remains closed, but trust in remote authorisation
collapses and later Seed communities require stronger plural verification.

## The Silence Breach of Relay Twelve

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Type:** Custody War incident

**Verified V11 basis:** Chapter 18 contrasts Lamps that require silence with
Lamps that require resonance; Chapter 21 describes forced access and data
attacks. V11 does not join these into a Relay Twelve incident.

An outside order forces a high-energy witness transmission through Relay
Twelve, claiming that untransmitted identities are being abandoned. The local
keepers argue the signal itself destabilises fragile relation fields.

**Linked site:** [The Pale Reef of Relay Twelve](scattered-lamps.md#the-pale-reef-of-relay-twelve)

**Consequence:** Some names are broadcast successfully; other uncertainty
structures are damaged. Both sides preserve evidence supporting their own
accusation.

## The Redirection of the Ash Convoy

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Type:** Custody War incident

**Verified V11 basis:** opposing Pallbearer orders redirect the same sealed
convoy without breaking vibration law.

Two matter-custody orders claim the same convoy. Neither side wants to damage
the burdens, so the conflict becomes a slow contest of route authority, frame
control, and lawful handoff rather than conventional battle.

**Linked site:** [The Ash Roads of Transfer Cluster K-6](scattered-lamps.md#the-ash-roads-of-transfer-cluster-k-6)

**Consequence:** The burdens survive, but their route provenance fractures into
incompatible records that later communities cannot fully reconcile.

## The Drowned Marker Seizure

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Type:** Custody War incident

**Verified V11 basis:** conflicts in reliquary coral seas involve
pressure-adapted custodians removing route markers from towers claimed by rival
faiths.

A rival authority removes submerged route markers from Pelagic Vault Seven,
claiming the markers unlawfully bind later transfers to a dead spatial
doctrine. Local custodians treat removal as destruction of historical relation.

**Linked site:** [The Drowned Threshold of Pelagic Vault Seven](scattered-lamps.md#the-drowned-threshold-of-pelagic-vault-seven)

**Consequence:** The recovered markers survive physically, but their original
currents, depths, and approach geometry cannot be recreated elsewhere.

## The Index Without Uncertainty

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Type:** Custody War atrocity

**Verified V11 basis:** V11 separately describes an archive copied without its
uncertainty fields and memory fields poisoned by false certainty. Their
combination into this incident and its later consequences is DRAFT.

A copied identity archive is deliberately simplified so every record resolves
to one answer. The copy is easier to use and spreads rapidly. Its missing
uncertainty fields are discovered only after it has become the basis for
transfers across multiple sites.

**Consequence:** Later custody decisions may be internally consistent but
historically untrustworthy. No surviving authority can fully reconstruct which
corrections were lost.
