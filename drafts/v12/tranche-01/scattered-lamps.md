# DRAFT — Scattered Lamp Cultures, Tranche 01

These are candidate extensions from the V12 working pack, not recovered canon.
Every Version 11 basis below is a pack assertion marked
**SOURCE-UNAVAILABLE** until the manuscript can be inspected.

## The Pale Reef of Relay Twelve

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Inheritance:** Memory, Structure

**Pack-stated V11 basis:** a Lamp whose outer hull became a pale archive reef,
with exposed radiator fins resembling frozen ribs.

A memory-centred continuity station whose cooling structure gradually became
architecture, habitat, and sacred boundary. Its keepers treat quiet operation
as proof of careful custody because vibration and signal noise once damaged
fragile identity systems.

Distinctive features:

- pale archive-glass growths layered around the old relay hull;
- long radiator fins read by later generations as ancestral ribs;
- identity pools that preserve contradiction rather than force one answer; and
- low-noise movement rules that may later resemble the claimed Quiet Route.

**Built-in danger:** Silence can preserve fragile records while isolating them
from correction.

## The Ash Roads of Transfer Cluster K-6

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Inheritance:** Remains, Structure

**Pack-stated V11 basis:** a Lamp inside a dust-choked asteroid where burden
routes were cut through mineral ash until the corridors resembled eroded
cathedrals.

A matter-first Lamp built through a mined asteroid. Burden movement remains
physical and slow. Route gradients, resting thresholds, and load scars become
the culture's most trusted historical evidence.

Distinctive features:

- mineral-ash transfer roads cut deep through the asteroid;
- burden frames rebuilt rather than replaced;
- rest thresholds positioned by measured vibration risk; and
- custodians who treat physical carrying as an audit trail.

**Built-in danger:** Material fidelity may be mistaken for complete identity.

## The Drowned Threshold of Pelagic Vault Seven

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Inheritance:** Structure, Memory

**Pack-stated V11 basis:** a Lamp above a drowned moon whose lower vaults
opened into black water and whose preservation towers became coral-like
frameworks for pressure-adapted custodians.

A flooded custody complex whose inhabitants adapt to pressure, darkness, and
submerged access. Here, approach is part of truth: a record removed from its
depth, route, and threshold is considered dangerously incomplete.

Distinctive features:

- black-water archive shafts;
- coral-like preservation towers grown around older machinery;
- pressure-adapted Threshold Walkers; and
- route markers readable through touch, pressure, and current.

**Built-in danger:** Place can become so sacred that necessary transfer becomes
nearly impossible.

## The Unopened Spring of Seed Reserve Thirty-One

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Inheritance:** Seed, Ritual

**Pack-stated V11 basis:** Seed Lamps that keep embryos viable after their
original release conditions become obsolete.

A seed reserve whose activation rules outlive the civilisation that wrote them.
Its culture grows around maintaining viability while debating whether any
surviving authority can honestly declare the required future conditions
satisfied.

Distinctive features:

- dormant biological libraries arranged by release condition;
- calendars based on repeated readiness tests;
- trial ecologies allowed to grow but not become settlements; and
- ceremonies that remain functional contamination-control procedures.

**Built-in danger:** Perfect preservation of possibility can become permanent
refusal to allow life.

## The Corridor of No Arrival

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Inheritance:** Ritual, Structure

**Pack-stated V11 basis:** route Lamps maintaining beacon corridors that no
vessel had used for longer than their own recorded history.

A route-centred culture that continues maintaining a transfer corridor after
its traffic disappears. Correct passage becomes the inheritance itself:
markers are repaired, gradients checked, and receiving intervals opened even
when nothing arrives.

Distinctive features:

- beacons maintained for destinations that no longer answer;
- route recitations derived from checksum and handoff procedure;
- population movement organised around maintenance windows; and
- a taboo against shortening a route merely because its purpose is forgotten.

**Built-in danger:** Procedure may survive after every reason for it has
changed.

## The Broken Frame Concordance at Transfer Site Nine

- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Inheritance:** Remains, Memory, Pattern

**Pack-stated V11 basis:** mixed sites, damaged burden capsules, fragmentary
records, and a later danger of purifying mixed inheritance into single-doctrine
systems.

A rare mixed Lamp formed around a disaster site where damaged burden frames,
partial names, and broken relation fields could not be reconciled honestly. Its
keepers preserve fragments side by side rather than constructing a false
single account.

Distinctive features:

- damaged frames displayed with their uncertainty records intact;
- plural custody councils rather than one inheritance authority;
- pattern reconstructions labelled as models rather than replacements; and
- deliberate preservation of unresolved contradiction.

**Built-in danger:** Plural caution can become paralysis when urgent action is
required.
