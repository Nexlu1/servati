# GitHub-First Naming Decision

## Search result

Before creating a SERVATI naming tool, GitHub was searched and licences were
checked.

- [`ironarachne/namegen@472ac3fe`](https://github.com/ironarachne/namegen/tree/472ac3fe67c734e89654dcad0293f6924fd2b87b)
  is Apache-2.0 and usable, but its list-based fictional human names do not fit
  late SERVATI terminology.
- `mewo2/naming-language` and `Tw1ddle/MarkovNameGenerator` remain quarantined
  because no clear reusable licence was established.
- The GPL-3.0 Lexurgy tool is suitable for a later, external historical-sound-
  change pass once actual language families have been designed.

## Tranche 01 decision

Do not build or import a name generator. Use translated functional names that
could descend from registry labels, route numbers, safety terms, maintenance
procedures, and environmental descriptions. Avoid random apostrophes, generic
alien syllables, and ornamental space-opera naming.
